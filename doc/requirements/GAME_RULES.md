# **PUSH: The Card Game**

**Players:** 2 or more

**Ages:** 9+

**Rounds:** 5

## **The Goal of the Game**

The goal of "Push" is to have the **lowest** score at the end of 5 rounds. You do this by getting rid of the cards in your hand before your opponents do\!

## **What You Need**

* **2 Standard Decks of Cards** (including the Jokers).  
* This makes a total of **108 cards**.  
* **Wild Cards:** All **2s** and all **Jokers** are wild. This means there are 12 wild cards in the deck\! A wild card can be used as any number or suit you need.  
  * *The "No Limits" Rule:* There are no restrictions on how many wild cards you can use in a Set or a Run. You can even make a Set entirely out of wilds\! Just remember, they are worth a massive 20 penalty points if you get caught with them at the end of a round.

## **Important Words to Know**

Before you play, you need to know the two ways to group your cards:

* **Sets:** 3 or more cards of the *same number*.  
  * *Example:* Three 4s, or two Kings and a Joker.  
* **Runs:** 4 or more cards of the *same suit in number order*.  
  * *Example:* 4, 5, 6, and 7 of Hearts.  
  * *Note on Aces:* Aces can be low (A, 2, 3, 4\) or high (J, Q, K, A), but they cannot wrap around (you cannot do K, A, 2, 3).  
* **"Getting on the Board":** This means you have successfully placed your required Sets or Runs face-up on the table for that round.

## **How to Set Up**

1. Shuffle all 108 cards together.  
2. Deal **10 cards** to each player.  
3. Place the remaining cards face-down in the center to create the **Draw Pile**.  
4. Flip the top card of the Draw Pile face-up next to it. This starts the **Discard Pile**.  
   * *Empty Deck Rule:* If the Draw Pile runs out before a round is over, use the Discard Pile as follows.
     * **Three or more cards:** Leave the top card. Shuffle the rest face-down to make a new Draw Pile. The player whose turn it is draws one card.
     * **Two cards:** Shuffle them. Give one to the player whose turn it is and one to the next player. This is rare, because almost every card is already in a hand or on the table. With three or more players, only those two players receive a card.
     * **One card:** It goes to the player whose turn it is.
     * **No cards:** There is nothing to draw.
     * If the Draw Pile still has cards, draw those one at a time. A short Discard Pile does not change that. One card on the Draw Pile and one on the Discard Pile means the current player takes the Draw Pile card, and the Discard Pile card waits for a later draw.
     * Two cards still on the Draw Pile are not dealt out together.

## **How to Play Your Turn**

When it is your turn, you must follow these steps in order:

### **1\. The Draw (or "The Push")**

You start your turn by looking at the face-up card on the Discard Pile. You have two choices:

* **Take It:** Pick up the discard and add it to your hand.  
* **Push It:** If you don't want the discard, you can "push" it to the next player.  
  * That player *must* take the discard into their hand, AND they must draw one extra penalty card from the top of the Draw Pile. (They cannot refuse or push the cards to the next person; they must wait for their normal turn to play).  
  * After you push the card to them, YOU draw the top card from the Draw Pile to start your turn.

### **2\. Getting on the Board (Melding)**

If you have the right cards in your hand, you can "get on the board" by placing them face-up on the table.

* You **must** meet the minimum requirement for the round all at once (see *The 5 Rounds* below).  
* You can put down *more* than the minimum if you have them. For example, if the round requires a Set of 3, you can put down a Set of 5 if you want\!

### **3\. Adding to Other Cards (Hitting) & Stealing Wilds**

**Rule:** *You can only do this step IF you are already on the board\!*

Once you have played your required cards, you can get rid of extra cards by adding them to Sets or Runs already on the table.

* *Adding:* If another player has a Set of three 6s, you can add your 6 to their pile. If someone has a Run of the 4, 5, 6, and 7 of Diamonds, you can add an 8 of Diamonds to their Run.  
* **Stealing Wild Cards:** If you see a wild card being used in a Run or Set anywhere on the table, and you have the *real* card it is replacing, you can swap them\! (Example: An opponent used a Joker as the 3 of Diamonds in a Run. You can place your real 3 of Diamonds there and take the Joker).  
  * *The Catch:* You **must** put the stolen wild card into your hand, and you **cannot** use it until your *next* turn\! This is a huge risk because if someone wins before it gets back to you, you are stuck with a 20-point penalty.

### **4\. Discard**

To end your turn, you must place one card from your hand face-up onto the Discard Pile.

* *Quick Discard:* You are allowed to pick up a card at the start of your turn and immediately discard that exact same card at the end of your turn (which is a great way to dump a high-point card like an Ace).  
* **The Safe Discard Rule:** If you are *not* on the board yet, you are **not allowed** to discard a card that could be played anywhere on the table.  
  * **The Penalty Draw:** If every single card in your hand is playable on the board, you cannot discard\! You must continuously draw one card at a time from the Draw Pile until you draw a "safe" card that *cannot* be played on the board. You then discard that safe card to end your turn.  
  * *Example:* You have 2 cards left, but both could be played on an opponent's Set. You must draw from the deck. It takes you 3 draws before finding a card that doesn't fit anywhere on the board. You discard it, ending your turn with 4 cards stuck in your hand.

## **The 5 Rounds**

Each round has a specific goal you must reach to "get on the board." You cannot play any cards until you can play exactly what the round asks for. Note: Both decks are fully reshuffled between every round\!

* **Round 1:** Two Sets of 3  
* **Round 2:** One Set of 3 **AND** One Run of 4  
* **Round 3:** Two Runs of 4  
* **Round 4:** Three Sets of 3  
* **Round 5:** One Set of 3 **AND** One Run of 7

## **Ending a Round & Scoring**

A round ends immediately when one player gets rid of all the cards in their hand. That player wins the round and gets **0 points**.

* *Note:* You do not need a final discard to win. You can win by playing all your remaining cards onto the board, or by playing all but one and discarding your final safe card.

Everyone else must count the points still left in their hands:

* **Cards 2 through 9:** 5 points each  
* **Cards 10 through King:** 10 points each  
* **Aces:** 15 points each  
* **Wild Cards (2s & Jokers):** 20 points each

Write down everyone's score. At the end of the next round, add their new points to their old points. After Round 5, the player with the lowest total score is the winner\!
